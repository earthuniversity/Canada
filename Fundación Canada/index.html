EARTH University
Canada Foundation

The EARTH University Canada Foundation is a private foundation created in 2023 to:

1) Advance education by providing publicly available scholarships, bursaries, and other forms of financial assistance to students to attend post-secondary education institutions.

2) Support economic growth and leadership development by operating a program, inclusive of business training and support designed to enable youth to transition successfully into employment and entrepreneurial roles in their communities.

3) Protect the environment for the benefit of the public by conserving, or restoring ecosystems, and promoting behaviour changes to strengthen climate change mitigation, adaptation, and resilience.

4) Advance education by providing workshops, training programs, seminars, conferences, educational aids and related support on topics related to business development, economic development, skills development, entrepreneurship, agricultural production, marketing, financial literacy, career skills and gender equality.

5) Advance education by providing a professional development and/or leadership program that involves training, workshops and fellowships for post-secondary students and recent graduates which furthers their understanding of entrepreneurship and supports them in transitioning to employment.

Purpose 01

Scholarships & Financial Assistance

Advance education by providing publicly available scholarships, bursaries, and other forms of financial assistance to students to attend post-secondary education institutions.

Purpose 02

On-the-Job Training & Workshops

Advance education by providing on-the-job training complemented by ongoing educational workshops and training sessions to post-secondary students and recent graduates whose studies are focused on agriculture, the environment, and/or agribusiness.

Purpose 03

Poverty Relief & Sustainable Agriculture

Relieve poverty in developing countries by providing training programs and related support on sustainable agriculture, business development, marketing, and financial literacy to individuals in need.

Purpose 04

Fund Management & Qualified Donees

Receive and maintain a fund or funds and to apply all or part of the principal and income therefrom, from time to time, to qualified donees as defined in subsection 149.1(1) of the Income Tax Act (Canada).

Purpose 05

Incidental & Ancillary Activities

Undertake activities ancillary and incidental to the attainment of the above mentioned charitable purposes.
